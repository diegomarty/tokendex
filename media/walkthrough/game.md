# Hatch, evolve, catch

**Your companion** is a starter you pick, then any species you have obtained or an egg (a free
one arrives every day; it hatches after 5M tokens of real work). The tokens it sees fill its
species' bar: a full bar _registers_ the species in your Pokédex and evolves it into the next
form. A registered species keeps going toward its shiny form.

**Wild Pokémon** appear as you work — the first one after just 500k tokens. They wait quietly
in a queue (a badge on the Tokendex icon counts them; only a shiny or a legendary will ever
toast, at most once per hour). When one is waiting it walks into the scene on Home: pick a
ball, throw, and watch the wobbles. A miss can make it flee. A first catch obtains the species
and fills 5% of its bar; a duplicate pays tokens.

A fresh save starts with **5 Poké Balls** — the first encounter is on the house.
