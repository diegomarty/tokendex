# Your companion lives in the status bar

Look at the bottom-right of the window: **Tokendex** shows your official limit usage (or
today's tokens) next to your companion's mood.

- `$(circle-outline)` an egg, incubating on your real AI spend
- `$(zap)` working — tokens are flowing
- `$(flame)` a blazing session

**Hover it** for the full breakdown: today, this month, per-CLI usage with burn rate, official
limit windows — and your companion's portrait.

Everything is read from your **local logs**. Nothing ever leaves your machine.
