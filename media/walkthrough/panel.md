# One scene for the whole game

The panel opens from the activity bar (the Tokendex icon) or by clicking the status bar item.

At the top of **Home** is the device's lid: your balance, and today's and this month's usage
with lights for the official limits (open it for the details). Below, your trainer stands with
your companion, and drawers sum up the rest in one line each: eggs, challenges, wild Pokémon,
finds, the legendary trail, your streak and the shop.

The other tabs:

- **Shop** — spend your accumulated tokens on Poké Balls, berries, eggs, Rare Candy, a Mint, the
  Shiny Charm
- **Bag** — use what you own
- **Pokédex** — every species' registration, the Shinydex, medals and your catch history
- **Settings** — language, your trainer sprite, save export/import
