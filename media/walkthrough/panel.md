# One scene for the whole game

The panel opens from the activity bar (the Tokendex icon) or by clicking the status bar item.

At the top of **Home**, your trainer stands with your companion. Below them: today's and this
month's usage, official limit windows as bars, and the per-CLI breakdown.

The other tabs:

- **Shop** — spend your accumulated tokens on Poké Balls, Rare Candy, a Mint, the Shiny Charm
- **Bag** — use what you own
- **Pokédex** — everything you have raised or caught
- **Settings** — language, your trainer sprite, save export/import
