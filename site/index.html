How it works

Every token is experience. Every session is a walk in the tall grass.

Pick a starter from generations 1 to 5, keep coding, and the tokens your AI tools log become its experience. Wild Pokémon walk into the editor while you work. Nothing ever interrupts you.

Raise

The tokens your companion sees fill its species' bar. A full bar registers it in the Pokédex and evolves it through its real evolution line. Eggs hatch at 5M tokens, one free a day.

Catch

Wild Pokémon appear as you code, the first after just 500k tokens. Every ball shows its real odds, computed with the Gen IV catch formula on the species' true capture rate. Miss, and it may get angry or flee. Shinies are 1 in 64.

Collect

649 species, Kanto to Unova, each with a full Pokédex entry: genus, types, base stats, the Pokédex text and its evolution line. Medals, regions and daily challenges along the way. A full Pokédex is a road of one to two years.

Questions

FAQ

Does Tokendex upload my code or my AI usage anywhere?

No. Tokendex only reads the log files the AI CLIs already keep on your disk, and everything stays on your machine. The only network calls fetch Pokémon sprites and species data from PokéAPI, and they are cached locally.

Which AI coding tools does Tokendex track?

Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Grok, Antigravity, Cursor, GitHub Copilot (the CLI and Copilot Chat inside VS Code), OpenCode, Hermes and Kiro. Providers you do not use never appear.

Does it slow down VS Code?

No. Scanning runs in a worker thread, never on the extension host. On a real 1.1 GB corpus the first scan takes about 6 seconds once, a warm start about 170 ms, and each refresh 90 to 115 ms.

Does it work in VSCodium, Cursor or Windsurf?

Yes. Tokendex is published on Open VSX as well as the VS Code Marketplace, and ships as a single .vsix for every platform with no native modules.

Is Tokendex free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT licence. It is a fan project, not affiliated with Nintendo, Creatures Inc. or GAME FREAK, and no Pokémon sprites are bundled.
